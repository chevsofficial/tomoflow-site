Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The web timer works entirely in your browser.

Where is my data?

Settings and Pro unlock live in this browser only.

How does Pro work?

One-time purchase via Stripe (~$21). After checkout, Pro unlocks the full theme library and extra characters on this browser. It is not a subscription.

How do I restore Pro after clearing site data?

On the home page, open TomoFlow Pro and use Restore Pro with the email on your Stripe receipt. We’ll check for a paid Pro checkout and email you a restore link. Open it in the browser you want to unlock. You can also email support@tomoflow.app with your receipt.

Do Bitcoin tips unlock Pro?

No. Tips are optional support only and never unlock Pro.
