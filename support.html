Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The web timer works entirely in your browser.

Where is my data?

Settings and Pro unlock live in localStorage on this device/browser.

How does Pro work?

One-time purchase via Stripe (~$21). After checkout, return to the site with ?pro=1 so this browser unlocks extra themes.

Do Bitcoin tips unlock Pro?

No. Tips are optional support only.

I cleared my browser and lost Pro

Email support with your Stripe receipt and we can help you re-apply the unlock.
