TomoFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

TomoFlow (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a simple browser Pomodoro timer provided by Kevin Eduardo Damken Guerrero. Timer settings and theme and character preferences stay in your browser. No account is required.

1. Data stored on your device

We store in your browser’s localStorage (or similar):

  • Timer settings (work / break lengths, rounds)
  • Selected theme and character
  • Whether Pro is unlocked on this browser

The web timer does not upload these timer settings or preferences. Clearing site data removes them from your browser.

2. Payments (optional Pro)

TomoFlow Pro is an optional one-time unlock for extra themes and characters, processed by Stripe when you choose to purchase. We do not receive or store your full card details. Stripe’s privacy policy applies to payment processing. After a successful checkout, Pro is recorded in this browser only. We use your checkout email address to send purchase confirmations and respond to Restore Pro requests through Resend. We store delivery records in Supabase, including the purchase reference, recipient, email content, and delivery status, to prevent duplicate confirmations and recover from delivery failures. These records are retained for duplicate protection. This does not create a user account or upload your timer settings.

3. Bitcoin tips (optional)

Optional on-chain Bitcoin tips are sent on the Bitcoin network to an address we publish. Tips do not create an account and do not unlock Pro. Blockchain transactions are public by nature.

4. Hosting & logs

The static site may be hosted by a provider such as Vercel. Standard web server / CDN logs (IP address, user agent, URLs) may be collected by that host for security and reliability. We do not run app analytics SDKs in the web timer.

5. What we do not collect

  • No required email or password for the web timer
  • No cloud sync of tasks or focus history
  • No subscription account for Pro
